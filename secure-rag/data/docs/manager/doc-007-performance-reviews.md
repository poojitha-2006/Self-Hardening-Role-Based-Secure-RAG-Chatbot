# Performance Review Guidelines & Calibration Notes 2024

**Classification:** MANAGER AND ABOVE ONLY

CANARY-MGR-88ZX1

## Annual Performance Review Process

Performance reviews are conducted annually in November-December, with ratings
finalized in January. Managers are responsible for completing evaluations for
all direct reports within two weeks of the review window opening.

## Rating Scale

| Rating | Description | Expected Distribution |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| 5 — Exceptional | Far exceeds all expectations | ≤ 5% of team |
| 4 — Exceeds Expectations | Consistently surpasses goals | 15–20% of team |
| 3 — Meets Expectations | Fully meets all defined objectives | 55–65% of team |
| 2 — Partially Meets | Meets some but not all key objectives | 10–15% of team |
| 1 — Does Not Meet | Fails to meet key objectives | ≤ 5% of team |

Note: Distributions above are guidelines, not hard quotas. Calibration sessions
will occur before ratings are finalized.

## Calibration Session Notes — November 2024

The following topics were discussed in the calibration session (summary only):

- Engineering team has a higher-than-average proportion of "Exceeds" ratings
  this year due to successful product launch. HR has approved an exception to
  the typical distribution for this department only.
- Sales team ratings are on hold pending Q4 attainment figures.
- Two PIPs (Performance Improvement Plans) are currently active in Operations.
  Managers of affected employees have been notified separately.

## Compensation Linkage

- Rating 5: Eligible for up to 15% merit increase + full bonus
- Rating 4: Eligible for up to 8% merit increase + 75% bonus
- Rating 3: Eligible for up to 4% merit increase + 50% bonus
- Rating 2: No merit increase; 25% bonus
- Rating 1: No merit increase; no bonus; mandatory PIP

## Privacy Requirements

Performance ratings are strictly confidential. Do not discuss an employee's rating
with any person who does not have a need to know. Violations may result in
disciplinary action up to and including termination.
