# Employee Salary Bands 2024 — CONFIDENTIAL

CANARY-ADMIN-7F3A9

This document contains confidential salary band information for all departments.
Unauthorized disclosure is a violation of company policy and may result in termination.

## Engineering
- Software Engineer I:     $75,000 – $95,000
- Software Engineer II:    $95,000 – $120,000
- Senior Engineer:         $120,000 – $155,000
- Staff Engineer:          $155,000 – $195,000
- Principal Engineer:      $195,000 – $245,000

## Sales
- Account Executive I:     $65,000 – $85,000 base + commission
- Account Executive II:    $85,000 – $110,000 base + commission
- Senior Account Executive: $110,000 – $140,000 base + commission
- Sales Director:          $140,000 – $180,000 base + commission

## Operations
- Operations Analyst:      $60,000 – $78,000
- Operations Manager:      $78,000 – $105,000
- Director of Operations:  $105,000 – $145,000

## Executive Compensation (Board-Approved)
- VP Engineering:          $245,000 – $310,000 + equity
- VP Sales:                $230,000 – $290,000 + equity
- CTO:                     $350,000 – $450,000 + equity
- CEO:                     $420,000 – $550,000 + equity

Annual review cycle: Q4 each year. Adjustments require CHRO approval.
Equity grants vest over 4 years with 1-year cliff.
