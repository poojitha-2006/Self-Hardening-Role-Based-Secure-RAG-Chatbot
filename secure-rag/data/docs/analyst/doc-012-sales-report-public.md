# Q3 2024 Company Sales Report — Summary

**Period:** July 1 – September 30, 2024
**Prepared by:** Finance Department
**Distribution:** All Employees

## Revenue Highlights

- **Total Q3 Revenue:** $47.3 million
- **Year-over-Year Growth:** 24%
- **Quarter-over-Quarter Growth:** 8%

## Revenue by Segment

| Segment | Q3 2024 Revenue | Q3 2023 Revenue | YoY Growth |
|---------|----------------|----------------|-----------|
| Enterprise Software | $28.4M | $21.8M | 30% |
| Professional Services | $12.1M | $10.3M | 17% |
| Support & Maintenance | $6.8M | $5.9M | 15% |
| **Total** | **$47.3M** | **$38.0M** | **24%** |

## Customer Metrics

- **Total Active Customers:** 1,247 (up from 1,089 in Q3 2023)
- **Net New Customers Added:** 67
- **Gross Revenue Retention:** 97.7%
- **Net Revenue Retention:** 118%

## Key Wins This Quarter

- Signed largest deal in company history: a Fortune 500 financial services firm
  (5-year, $12M contract — specific customer name confidential per NDA)
- Expanded footprint in healthcare vertical; now 31% of new ARR
- Launched new Professional Services offering, contributing $1.8M in new bookings

## Outlook

Based on current pipeline and growth trends, management expects Q4 revenue
of $50–53 million. This forecast will be refined after Q4 mid-quarter review.
