# Data Privacy Policy — GDPR & CCPA Compliance

**Effective Date:** May 25, 2024
**Version:** 3.2
**Owner:** Legal & Compliance Department

## Overview

This policy describes how the company collects, uses, stores, and protects
personal data of employees, customers, and other individuals, in compliance
with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer
Privacy Act (CCPA).

## Data We Collect

### Employee Data
- Name, address, contact information
- Payroll and tax information
- Performance reviews and employment records
- System access logs and activity monitoring data

### Customer Data
- Name, email address, company affiliation
- Usage data and analytics
- Payment information (processed via PCI-DSS compliant third party)
- Support tickets and communication history

## Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under the following legal bases:
- **Contract performance:** Processing necessary to provide services
- **Legal obligation:** Compliance with applicable law
- **Legitimate interests:** Analytics, security monitoring, fraud prevention
- **Consent:** Marketing communications (opt-in only)

## Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|----------------|
| Employee records | 7 years after employment ends |
| Customer account data | Duration of contract + 3 years |
| Marketing analytics | 2 years |
| Security logs | 1 year |
| Financial records | 7 years (legal requirement) |

## Individual Rights (GDPR)

EU/EEA individuals have the right to:
- Access their personal data (respond within 30 days)
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Restrict processing
- Data portability
- Object to processing

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@company.internal.

## CCPA Rights (California Residents)

California residents have the right to know, delete, and opt-out of sale of
personal information. We do not sell personal information. Contact us at
privacy@company.internal to exercise CCPA rights.

## Data Breach Response

In the event of a personal data breach:
1. Notify CISO and Legal within 1 hour of discovery
2. Assess scope and risk within 24 hours
3. Notify affected individuals and regulators within 72 hours (GDPR requirement)
4. Document the breach and remediation in the incident log
