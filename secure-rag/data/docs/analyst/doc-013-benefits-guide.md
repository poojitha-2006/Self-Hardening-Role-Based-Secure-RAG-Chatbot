# Employee Benefits Guide 2024

**Distribution:** All Employees

## Health Insurance

The company offers three health insurance plans through BlueCross BlueShield:

### Plan Options
1. **Health Plus (PPO)** — Lowest deductible, highest premium
   - Monthly premium: $0 employee / $280 family contribution
   - Annual deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family
   - Out-of-pocket max: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family

2. **Health Select (HDHP)** — High deductible, HSA eligible
   - Monthly premium: $0 employee / $140 family contribution
   - Annual deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
   - HSA contribution: Company contributes $750/year (individual) or $1,500/year (family)

3. **Health Basic (HMO)** — Network-restricted, lowest out-of-pocket
   - Monthly premium: $0 employee / $200 family contribution
   - Annual deductible: $750 individual / $1,500 family
   - Referrals required for specialists

## Dental and Vision

- **Dental:** 100% preventive care covered; 80% basic restorative; 50% major restorative
- **Vision:** One eye exam per year covered; $200 frames/contacts allowance annually

## Retirement Benefits

- **401(k) Plan:** Company matches 100% of first 4% of salary contributed
- **Vesting:** Immediate vesting for employee contributions; 3-year graded vesting for company match
- **Auto-enrollment:** New employees auto-enrolled at 3% contribution after 30 days

## Paid Time Off

- **Vacation:** 15 days/year (Years 1-3); 20 days/year (Years 4+); 25 days/year (Years 8+)
- **Sick Leave:** 10 days/year (does not roll over)
- **Holidays:** 11 fixed holidays + 2 floating holidays per year
- **Parental Leave:** 12 weeks fully paid for primary caregiver; 4 weeks for secondary caregiver

## Additional Benefits

- Remote work stipend: $500/year for home office equipment
- Learning & Development: $2,000/year for approved courses, certifications, conferences
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Free confidential counseling, 6 sessions/year
- Commuter benefits: Pre-tax transit/parking up to IRS limits

## Open Enrollment

Open enrollment runs November 1–15 annually. Benefits elected take effect January 1.
Changes outside open enrollment require a qualifying life event. Contact HR at
hr@company.internal or ext. 5000 for assistance.
