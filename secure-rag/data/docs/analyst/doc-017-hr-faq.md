# Frequently Asked Questions — HR Portal

**Updated:** October 2024
**Contact:** hr@company.internal | Ext. 5000

## Payroll & Compensation

**Q: When is payday?**
A: Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. The payroll cutoff is the Wednesday
two weeks prior. If your pay date falls on a bank holiday, payment is made the
preceding business day.

**Q: How do I update my direct deposit information?**
A: Log in to Workday → Finances → Payment Elections. Changes take effect within
two pay periods. If you need immediate assistance, contact Payroll at payroll@company.internal.

**Q: What is the expense reimbursement process?**
A: Submit expenses through Expensify with receipts attached. Expenses must be
submitted within 60 days of incurring them. Manager approval is required.
Reimbursements are processed in the next payroll cycle after approval.

**Q: I received an incorrect paycheck. What do I do?**
A: Email payroll@company.internal with your employee ID, the date of the error,
and the amount. We will investigate and correct within 5 business days.

## Time Off

**Q: How do I request vacation?**
A: Submit a time-off request in Workday at least 5 business days in advance
(2 weeks for requests over 5 days). Your manager will approve or deny in Workday.

**Q: Can I carry over unused vacation?**
A: Up to 40 hours of unused vacation can be carried over to the following year.
Any hours above 40 are forfeited on January 1.

**Q: How do I report sick leave?**
A: Notify your manager before your scheduled start time (or as early as possible)
and log your absence in Workday as sick leave. No doctor's note required for
absences under 5 consecutive days.

## Benefits

**Q: When does health insurance coverage start?**
A: Coverage starts on the first of the month following your start date.

**Q: Who is my HR Business Partner?**
A: Find your assigned HRBP in the company directory on the intranet.
Engineering employees: Jane Morrison. Sales employees: Carlos Reyes. Operations: Pat Lin.

## General

**Q: Where can I find the employee handbook?**
A: The employee handbook is available on the company intranet under HR Resources.
A PDF version can be requested from hr@company.internal.

**Q: How do I report a workplace concern?**
A: You can report anonymously via the Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-ETHICS or
ethicshotline.company.com. You can also speak directly with HR or your HRBP.
