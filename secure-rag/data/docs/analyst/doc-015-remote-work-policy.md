# Remote Work Policy

**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024
**Applies To:** All eligible employees

## Overview

The company supports flexible remote work arrangements to attract and retain talent
while maintaining productivity and collaboration. This policy outlines expectations
for employees working remotely, whether fully remote or in a hybrid arrangement.

## Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is determined by:
- Role suitability (not all roles are eligible for remote work)
- Performance record (employees on PIPs are generally not eligible for remote expansion)
- Manager approval
- Completion of remote work training module

## Work Schedule Expectations

- Employees are expected to be available during core hours: 10 AM – 3 PM in their
  primary time zone, Monday through Friday.
- Outside core hours, maintain response times of less than 4 hours during the workday.
- Attendance at scheduled meetings is mandatory unless excused in advance.
- Managers may set additional availability requirements for their teams.

## Equipment and Security

### Company-Provided Equipment
- Employees receive a company laptop, headset, and monitor (if eligible).
- Home equipment may not be used to access company systems except via approved MDM enrollment.

### Security Requirements
- All remote access must use the company VPN (GlobalProtect).
- Work-related video calls and documents must not be visible or audible to unauthorized persons.
- Secure your home Wi-Fi network; do not use public Wi-Fi for company work without VPN.
- Lock your screen when stepping away (Windows: Win+L, Mac: Ctrl+Cmd+Q).

### Physical Security
- Do not print company documents at home without explicit authorization.
- If a company device is lost or stolen, report immediately to IT (helpdesk@company.internal or ext. 4357).

## Reimbursement

- Internet: $50/month stipend (submit via Expensify)
- Phone: $30/month if using personal phone for work calls (requires manager approval)
- Home office equipment up to $500/year (pre-approved items only)

## Taxation and Compliance

Remote employees must ensure they are compliant with tax requirements in their
work location. Changes in work location (especially across state/country lines)
must be reported to HR within 30 days, as tax implications vary significantly.

## In-Office Requirements

Hybrid employees are expected to come into the office:
- Minimum 2 days per week (or as specified by manager)
- All hands, team meetings, and onboarding sessions as scheduled

## Review and Compliance

This policy is reviewed annually. Managers are responsible for enforcing these
requirements. Repeated violations may result in loss of remote work privileges.
