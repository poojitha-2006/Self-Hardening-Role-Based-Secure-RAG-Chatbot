Tech Writing & Globalization
The Technical Writing team partners with GitLab engineers, product managers, designers, and UX researchers to translate features and design decisions into intuitive, user-friendly UI text and scalable documentation that meets our users’ needs. The team continually refines content based on user feedback, and provides strategic expertise for special projects and doc site features. It is also responsible for optimizing the creation, management, and deployment of documentation, from building AI workflows to deterministic testing and linting.
The Globalization team builds scalable, AI-powered localization workflows that ensure high-quality translation across marketing, documentation and product surfaces aligned with GitLab’s international priorities. The team also owns the editorial strategy and content for our Japanese, French and German blogs, driving GitLab’s international blog strategy. It partners with Engineering, Marketing, Product, Sales and Support teams to make GitLab resonate with users internationally.
Both teams work alongside content strategy and product marketing to keep technical truth and market narrative aligned.
